# AgriSahay AI

## AI-Based Agricultural Government Scheme Assistance System

### 📌 Project Overview
AgriSahay AI is an AI-powered agricultural assistance platform designed to help farmers discover government schemes, understand their eligibility, and access relevant agricultural information in a simple and user-friendly way.

The platform aims to reduce the difficulties farmers face in understanding government schemes, language barriers, and finding reliable information about agricultural support programs.

### 🎯 Problem Statement
Many farmers are unaware of the government schemes and financial assistance programs available to them. Complex official information, language barriers, lack of digital awareness, and difficulty finding reliable resources make it challenging for farmers to access the benefits they deserve.

### 💡 Proposed Solution
AgriSahay AI provides a centralized digital platform where farmers can:
- Explore agricultural government schemes.
- Understand scheme eligibility and benefits.
- Access agricultural assistance and information.
- Overcome language barriers through AI-powered guidance.
- Find relevant information without navigating multiple websites.

### ✨ Features
- **Government Scheme Information:** Provides information about agricultural government schemes and benefits.
- **AI Assistance:** Helps users understand agricultural information in a simplified manner.
- **Language-Friendly Support:** Makes information easier to understand for users who face language barriers.
- **Farmer-Friendly Interface:** Simple and accessible design for farmers.
- **Agricultural Support:** Helps farmers find relevant resources and assistance.
